# Privacy Policy for Focus Flow

**Last Updated:** September 19, 2026

Focus Flow ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with the Focus Flow mobile and web application.

---

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect
- **No Personal Identification:** Focus Flow does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
- **No Sensitive Personal Data:** We do not collect health data, biometric identifiers, or financial account credentials. All simulated revenue metrics or financial projections displayed within the application are strictly fictional for gamification and entertainment purposes.

### 2. Local-Only Storage
- All productivity records, including Pomodoro timer sessions, focus duration, custom task lists, priority ratings, and streak counters, are stored strictly on your local device (via browser LocalStorage/IndexedDB).
- This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

### 3. Optional Diagnostics & Crash Reporting
- To ensure app performance and stability, anonymous technical diagnostics (e.g., app version, operating system version, and anonymous crash stack traces) may be gathered through standard Google Play Console developer tools.
- These diagnostics contain no personal identifiers and are used exclusively to troubleshoot bugs.

### 4. In-App Purchases & Advertising
- If optional premium subscriptions or in-app purchases are enabled, transactions are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or payment details.
- Any advertising displays comply with Google Play Developer Program policies and do not engage in unauthorized cross-app tracking.

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
- Because your productivity and task data is stored solely on your device, you maintain complete ownership.
- You can clear all data at any time by wiping the app cache/data in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.

### 6. Children's Privacy
- Focus Flow does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
- We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date.
